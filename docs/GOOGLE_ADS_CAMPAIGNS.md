## TRACKING & OPTIMIZATION

1. **UTM Parameters** (use on all ads):
   - Search: `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=search`
   - Display: `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=display&utm_campaign=retargeting`
   - YouTube: `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=youtube&utm_campaign=video`

2. **A/B Testing:**
   - Test 3 headlines + 2 descriptions per ad group
   - Pause ads with CTR < 1%
   - Increase budget for ad groups with CTR > 5% and CPA < $5

3. **Negative Keywords:**
   - Add: free, hack, crack, torrent, illegal, cheat, bypass, fake, alternative, clone, github, github.com
   - Prevents wasted spend on irrelevant searches

4. **Schedule:**
   - Run ads 24/7 initially
   - After 2 weeks, analyze performance by hour and day
   - Increase bids during high-conversion hours (typically 09:00–18:00 PKT)

5. **Device Bidding:**
   - Mobile: +20% (majority of users in Pakistan/India use mobile)
   - Desktop: +10%
   - Tablet: -30%

6. **Location Bidding:**
   - Pakistan: +30%
   - India: +20%
   - UAE, Saudi Arabia, UK, USA: +10%
