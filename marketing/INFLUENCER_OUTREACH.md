## OUTREACH TIPS

1. **Personalize every email** — mention specific videos/posts they made
2. **Keep it short** — influencers get 100+ pitches per day
3. **Show value first** — lead with what they get, not what you want
4. **Be genuine** — no jargon, no corporate speak
5. **Follow up** — 60% of responses come from follow-ups, not first email
6. **Use Instagram DMs** — often more effective than email for creators
7. **Offer flexibility** — some prefer stories, some prefer long-form
