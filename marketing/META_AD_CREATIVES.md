## A/B TEST VARIATIONS

For each ad, create 2-3 variations:

1. **Headline test:** Short vs. long headline
2. **CTA test:** "Sign Up" vs. "Get Started" vs. "Download"
3. **Visual test:** Screenshot vs. animated demo vs. testimonial
4. **Audience test:** Interest-based vs. lookalike vs. retargeting

Run each variation with $10/day budget, kill losers after 3 days, scale winners.
