## 🎯 PRO TIPS FOR SOCIAL MEDIA SUCCESS

### General Tips
1. **Post consistently** — 3-5 times per week minimum
2. **Use video content** — Reels/TikToks get 10x more engagement
3. **Engage with comments** — Reply within 1 hour
4. **Use hashtags strategically** — Mix popular and niche tags
5. **Post at optimal times** — 9 AM, 1 PM, 8 PM (local time)
6. **Collaborate with influencers** — Tech reviewers, developers, creators
7. **Run contests** — "Create the best AI-generated video"
8. **Share user success stories** — Social proof is powerful

### Platform-Specific Tips

**Facebook:**
- Post in relevant groups (developers, tech enthusiasts)
- Use Facebook Ads to target tech audiences
- Create events for webinars/tutorials

**Instagram:**
- Use all 30 hashtags (mix of sizes)
- Post Reels daily for algorithm favor
- Use Stories with polls and Q&As
- Collaborate with tech influencers

**TikTok:**
- Keep videos under 60 seconds
- Use trending sounds and effects
- Post 2-3 times per day
- Engage with comments immediately

**WhatsApp:**
- Broadcast to multiple groups
- Share success stories and testimonials
- Offer exclusive WhatsApp-only deals
- Create broadcast lists by interest

**YouTube:**
- Optimize titles and descriptions with keywords
- Create thumbnail with face + text
