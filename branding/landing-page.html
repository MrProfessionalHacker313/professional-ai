❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Professional AI

What is Professional AI? +

Professional AI is the world's most powerful all-in-one AI platform. It combines code generation in every programming language, 8K media creation, advanced cybersecurity tools, multilingual support (40+ languages), and offline capabilities — all in one platform.

Is there a free plan available? +

Yes! Professional AI offers a generous free tier with 3 code prompts/day, 50 chat messages/day, 1 video + 10 images + 3 animations per day, and offline basic search. No credit card required.

What programming languages are supported? +

Professional AI supports 40+ programming languages including Python, JavaScript, Java, C++, Rust, Go, Swift, Kotlin, PHP, Ruby, TypeScript, C#, and many more. We also support all major frameworks like React, Django, Spring, .NET, etc.

Does Professional AI work offline? +

Yes! Professional AI offers comprehensive offline mode. You can search, login, and even write code without an internet connection. The free tier includes basic offline search, while paid plans offer full offline capabilities including coding and AI model execution.

What languages are supported for chat? +

Professional AI supports 40+ languages including Urdu, Hindi, Bengali, Arabic, English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Russian, Turkish, Persian, and many more. You can chat, code, and create content in your native language.

What are the payment options? +

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and local payment methods. For Pakistan, we offer special PKR pricing with local payment options. All paid plans come with a 3-day free trial.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime? +

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time with no questions asked. Your access will continue until the end of your billing period. We also offer a 3-day free trial for all paid plans, so you can test before committing.

Is my data secure with Professional AI? +

Absolutely. We use military-grade encryption, vault encryption for sensitive data, and follow zero-knowledge architecture principles. Your data is never shared with third parties. We also offer advanced security tools like RAT detection and red-team suites for MAX plan users.
