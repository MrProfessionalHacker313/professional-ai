## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit websites or use applications. They are widely used to make websites and applications work more efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to site owners.

## 2. How We Use Cookies

[COMPANY_NAME] ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on the Professional AI platform ("Service"). This Cookie Policy explains what cookies we use, why we use them, and how you can control them.

## 3. Types of Cookies We Use

### 3.1 Essential Cookies (Strictly Necessary)
These cookies are required for the Service to function properly. They enable core functionality such as:

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| `session_id` | User authentication and session management | Session |
| `csrf_token` | Security protection against cross-site request forgery | Session |
| `auth_token` | Maintain login state | 30 days |
| `preferences` | Store user interface preferences | 1 year |

**These cookies cannot be disabled** as they are necessary for the Service to operate.

### 3.2 Analytics Cookies
We use **Google Analytics** to understand how users interact with the Service. These cookies collect anonymized information about:

- Pages visited
- Time spent on pages
- Navigation patterns
- Device and browser type

**Google Analytics Cookies:**
- `_ga` — Distinguishes unique users (2 years)
- `_gid` — Distinguishes unique users (24 hours)
- `_gat` — Throttles request rate (1 minute)

**Opt-out:** You can opt out of Google Analytics using the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

### 3.3 Functional Cookies
These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization:

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| `theme_pref` | Remember dark/light mode preference | 1 year |
| `language` | Store language preference | 1 year |
| `editor_config` | Remember code editor settings | 1 year |

### 3.4 Marketing Cookies
We do not currently use marketing/advertising cookies. If we introduce marketing features in the future, we will update this policy and obtain your consent.

## 5. Local Storage and Similar Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use:
- **Local Storage:** Store larger amounts of data locally (e.g., chat history, editor state)
- **Session Storage:** Temporary data for the current session
- **IndexedDB:** Store structured data for offline functionality

These technologies function similarly to cookies but may store more data. You can clear them through your browser settings.

## 6. Mobile Applications

Our mobile applications may use:
- **Device identifiers:** For analytics and personalization
- **Local storage:** For offline functionality
- **Push notification tokens:** For sending notifications

You can manage these through your device settings.

## 7. How to Manage Cookies

### 7.1 Browser Settings
You can control cookies through your browser settings:

| Browser | Cookie Settings |
|---------|----------------|
| **Chrome** | Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data |
| **Firefox** | Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data |
| **Safari** | Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data |
| **Edge** | Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Cookies |

### 7.2 Cookie Options
Most browsers allow you to:
- Accept or reject all cookies
- Accept cookies from specific sites only
- Delete existing cookies
- Set preferences for specific types of cookies

### 7.3 Impact of Disabling Cookies
Disabling essential cookies may prevent you from:
- Logging into your account
- Maintaining session state
- Using certain features of the Service

Disabling analytics cookies will not affect Service functionality but will limit our ability to improve the Service.

## 8. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers support "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals. Currently, there is no industry standard for how DNT signals should be interpreted. We do not currently respond to DNT signals but will review this policy as standards evolve.
