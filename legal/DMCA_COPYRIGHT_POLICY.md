## 4. Filing a DMCA Takedown Notice

To file a DMCA takedown notice, the copyright owner or their authorized agent must provide a written communication containing:

### 4.1 Required Information
1. **Identification of the copyrighted work** claimed to have been infringed, or, if multiple works, a representative list
2. **Identification of the infringing material** to be removed, with sufficient detail for us to locate it (URLs, content identifiers, etc.)
3. **Contact information** of the complaining party:
   - Full legal name
   - Physical address
   - Telephone number
   - Email address
4. **Statement of good faith belief** that the use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law
5. **Statement under penalty of perjury** that the information in the notice is accurate and that the complaining party is authorized to act on behalf of the owner
6. **Physical or electronic signature** of the copyright owner or authorized agent

### 4.2 Submission
Send the complete DMCA notice to our designated agent at:
- **Email:** [DMCA_EMAIL]
- **Physical Mail:** [COMPANY_ADDRESS], Attn: DMCA Copyright Agent

## 5. Counter-Notification Procedure

If you believe your content was removed in error, you may file a counter-notification.

### 5.1 Required Information
1. **Identification of the removed material** and its former location
2. **Statement under penalty of perjury** that you have a good faith belief the material was removed by mistake or misidentification
3. **Your contact information** (name, address, phone, email)
4. **Consent to jurisdiction** of the Federal District Court for the judicial district in which your address is located (or any judicial district where we may be found)
5. **Statement that you will accept service of process** from the person who provided the original takedown notice
6. **Physical or electronic signature**

### 5.2 Processing
Upon receiving a valid counter-notification, we will:
- Forward the counter-notification to the original complainant
- Wait 10-14 business days for the complainant to file a court action
- Restore the removed content if no court action is filed within the waiting period

## 6. Repeat Infringer Policy

### 6.1 Termination
We will terminate, in appropriate circumstances and at our sole discretion, the accounts of users who are determined to be repeat infringers.

### 6.2 Definition
A "repeat infringer" is defined as a user who has received two or more valid DMCA takedown notices for separate infringing activities.

## 7. False Claims

### 7.1 Liability
Anyone who knowingly materially misrepresents that material is infringing may be liable for damages, including costs and attorneys' fees, under Section 512(f) of the DMCA.

### 7.2 Consequences
We reserve the right to terminate the accounts of users who repeatedly file false DMCA notices.

## 8. Processed Content and AI-Generated Material

### 8.1 AI-Generated Content
Users retain ownership of content they create using the Service. If you believe AI-generated content infringes your copyright, contact us with details.

### 8.2 Processed Content
Content submitted by users for AI processing is processed transiently and not permanently stored by us beyond retention periods specified in our Privacy Policy.

## 9. Chilling Effects and Over-Removal

### 9.1 Careful Review
We review all DMCA notices carefully to avoid over-removal of non-infringing content.

### 9.2 Restoration
If content is incorrectly removed, we will promptly restore it upon receiving a valid counter-notification.
